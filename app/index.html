0% of the way to the platinum

One trophy can pass you by — read this before Chapter 1 ends

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida — is the only trophy two guides flag as possibly missable. Its first meditation is offered at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 and is gone for good once that chapter ends. Meditate there before you finish Chapter 1. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one more before the final mission.

Flagged by PowerPyx’s GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap and by GTABase. Full source list below.

    Roadmap to the platinum

    PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours, with a single playthrough enough. There are no online trophies, no difficulty settings and no mission medals — the set is long rather than hard. Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so leave them alone.

    Stage 1 · The story

    60–80 hours

    Play all five chapters and the named missions, work the stranger strands as they turn up, and sample each activity type once. Meditate at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 while you still can.

      Stage 2 · 100% & the big counters

      About 100 hours

      Once the story is done, work the 100% Completion checklist (Progress, then Stats) and 70 of 80 challenges: stunts, shooting-range golds, races, collectibles and the long counters.

        Stage 3 · Cleanup

        Whatever is left

        Partner trophies, free-roam moments and anything still unchecked. The platinum pops the moment the last of the other 54 is earned.
