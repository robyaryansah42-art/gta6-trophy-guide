Read this first — the one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy two guides flag as possibly missable. The first meditation is offered at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 and becomes permanently unavailable after that chapter. Meditate there before you finish Chapter 1. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

Source: PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; GTABase. See Sources below.

Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours, with one playthrough enough. There are no online trophies, no difficulty settings and no mission medals — it is long, not hard. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off.

Stage 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

Finish all five chapters and the named missions, do the stranger strands as they appear, and try each activity type once. Meditate at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 before it is gone.

    Stage 2 — 100% Completion & challenges ~100 hours

    After the story, work the 100% Completion checklist (Progress, then Stats) and 70 of 80 challenges: stunts, shooting-range golds, races, collectibles and the big counters.

      Stage 3 — Cleanup

      Partner trophies, free-roam moments and anything left over. The platinum pops when the last of the other 54 is earned.
